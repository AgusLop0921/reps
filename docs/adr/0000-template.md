# ADR-NNNN: One-line title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The situation forcing a decision. Real constraints (time, knowledge, cost), not wishes.
If a specific fact drove the decision, it belongs here.

## Decision

What we decided, in one or two sentences. Past tense, affirmative.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A** — why not.
- **Option B** — why not.

## Consequences

What gets easier, what gets harder, what we are giving up. Include the annoying
consequences: an ADR that only lists upsides is badly written.
